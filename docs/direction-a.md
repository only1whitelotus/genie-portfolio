# The Living Studio — selected direction

Direction A was approved on 27 September 2026, with authority to make routine design and implementation decisions.

Reference lock: Dennis Snellenberg's confident typography and personal presentation is the primary compositional reference. Borrow only the idea of a spatial transformation from Lusion and the clear media/credit hierarchy from Ordinary Folk. Original client imagery supplies the visual evidence.

- Canvas: graphite #121316, cool white #F3F4F2, silver #CDD1D5.
- Action: signal blue #2457F5; never recolor client art.
- Type: self-hosted Manrope variable; IBM Plex Mono for small labels.
- Signature: a perspective frame moves between Design, Film and Code. DOM/CSS and GSAP keep it responsive and accessible without mandatory WebGL.
- Structure: work appears immediately after the opening; every discipline gets a featured project.
- Preserve project URLs, publication credits, existing media and the public email route.
- Omit unverified numerical outcomes, experience counters, launch dates and current FBL rules.
- Motion: native scrolling, fast functional transitions, visible motion toggle and OS preference. All enhancements have a readable static state.
- Build target: this approved reference lock; no additional concept-approval gate is required.

Content needing owner-provided assets or account configuration is tracked in the README. The contact form must never claim delivery when email infrastructure is unconfigured.
